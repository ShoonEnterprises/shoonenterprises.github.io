Privacy Policy — Shoon Enterprises App

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What this app does

This app publishes pins to the developer's own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API. It is for single, personal use only.

Data handling

  • The app accesses only the developer's own Pinterest account: the boards and pins they own.
  • Pin images, titles and descriptions are the developer's own marketing content.
  • The app does not collect, store or process personal data belonging to any other person.
  • No data is sold to third parties. No data is shared with third parties except Pinterest itself, as required to publish pins via its API.

Data retention

No user data is retained by the app beyond what is needed to complete a publishing request.
